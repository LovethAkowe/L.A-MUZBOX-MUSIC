In a world where music is more than just sound—it's emotion, identity, and culture—data can reveal the stories behind our favorite tracks. This dataset, a curated collection of streaming statistics, TikTok virality, and musical attributes, provides a fascinating look into how genres, artists, and songs resonate with listeners globally. 
STORY OF DATA
From the hard-hitting beats of EchoSync’s trap anthem to the mellow acoustic vibes of Sofia Carter's blues ballad, this dataset showcases the vast diversity in music tastes. Interestingly, while Retro Resonance's reggae hit from 1992 still garners significant daily streams, newer artists like Urban Rhapsody are riding high on TikTok trends, helping their songs stay on charts longer. Pop and electronic tracks exhibit high danceability and energy, correlating with strong TikTok virality and streaming longevity, while more acoustic and emotionally rich genres show varying levels of listener engagement.
